How these guides are written

Three rules, so you can rely on them:

  1. Only my own numbers. Anything I haven't measured myself is labeled as an estimate, not stated as fact.
  2. No income promises. Not here and not in my programs. What you can control is conversations and fulfillment.
  3. No legal advice. On marketing law, GDPR and tax, I write what's practice in my own projects in Germany – the binding assessment comes from a lawyer and a tax advisor.
