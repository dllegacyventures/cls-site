Claude for Business

I've been working with Claude almost daily since early 2025 – not as a chat window, but as a construction machine. This guide covers what it's good for, where ChatGPT is better, and what it actually costs.

A guide by Mario De Luca · Updated Oct 14, 2026 · Figures from my own projects · Auf Deutsch

In short: Claude is strong at long context, code and multi-step work on real files. ChatGPT is broader on images, voice and everyday tools. The real lever for businesses isn't the chat window but Claude Code in the terminal, because it works on files and systems instead of paragraphs.

Claude or ChatGPT: the honest comparison

I use both. The question isn't which model is “better”, but what you use it for.

TaskMy pickWhy
Building and refactoring softwareClaudekeeps large contexts together, works on real files instead of snippets
Working through long documentsClaudestays on track across a hundred pages
Sales copy in GermanClaudefewer marketing clichés, closer to how people actually talk
Images, voice, everyday toolsChatGPTwider selection right inside the product

The practical difference that matters day to day: Claude is more likely to admit it doesn't know something, and less likely to invent a subheading where one sentence will do. For sales copy in German, that's why I stuck with it.

Claude Code: the part hardly anyone uses

The chat window is the smallest use case. The lever is Claude Code – the same model, but in the terminal, with access to files, Git, commands and APIs. That turns “write me some code” into a worker that refactors an entire project, runs the tests and explains the change.

What I've built with it in my own company: a CRM portal, several websites with their own offers, a video editing pipeline, a sales dashboard on real daily numbers, and an internal operations assistant on a dedicated machine. No degree, no developer team – but no magic either: if you don't read what it does, you get software you don't understand.

The three rules that made the difference for me:

  1. Brief before code. First write down what “done” means – then let it build. Without acceptance criteria it delivers something plausible.
  2. A separate reviewer. A second pass with an empty context that only sees the change and the criteria. It finds what the builder would defend.
  3. No access to live data while building. Test data, separate permissions, and nothing goes to production without your own eyes on it.

What Claude costs

There are two ways to pay, and they're fundamentally different:

  • Subscription per person. A fixed monthly price, usage within limits. Right for employees who write, summarize and research.
  • API, pay as you go. Billed per token – roughly one and a half tokens per word, split into input and output. Right for everything that gets built into your own software.

Because prices change by model and over time, I won't give you a table that's wrong tomorrow. Instead, here's what drives the bill:

DriverEffectFix
Input lengthevery call pays for all the text sent alongonly send what's needed
Model choicesmall and large models are often several times apartsmall model for sorting, large one for thinking
Agent loopsevery step is a new callset a step limit, define a stop condition
Repeated contextthe same instructions in every calluse prompt caching – the big saving
Output lengthoutput costs more than inputask for a short, fixed output format

A measured number from my own company instead of an estimate: two complete software jobs through my own assembly line – specification, build, tests, review – cost 52 and 114 US dollars in model costs. That's the order of magnitude for cleanly scoped jobs. An agent without a step limit can blow past that in an afternoon. More in How much does an AI agent cost?

Data privacy: the four questions that matter

Not “is AI GDPR-compliant?”, but:

  1. Is there a data processing agreement? For the business plans of the big providers, yes – but it has to be signed and filed, not just exist.
  2. Are my inputs used for training? For business and API access, the providers say no; for free tiers it's often different. That's the point I re-read in writing for every project, because terms change.
  3. Which data goes in at all? The most effective measure isn't the contract but data minimization: replace names, shorten numbers, transcribe call content locally. In my setup, transcription runs on my own machine so calls never leave the building.
  4. Is it in your records of processing? A tool nobody registered is the most common real violation – not the model itself.

I'm not a lawyer and not a data protection officer. For a binding assessment of your specific case, you need both.

Claude in German

A lot of my copy is written in German, and Claude works in German without restrictions – interface, input, output. The difference isn't the language but the brief: ask for “an ad text” and you get ad-speak. Give it a voice – example sentences, banned words, sentence lengths – and you get something you can read out loud. That's why my copy templates always include a list of words that must not appear.

Frequently asked questions

Claude or ChatGPT for business?

For software, long documents and German sales copy I use Claude; for images, voice and the broad tool selection, ChatGPT. Many businesses run both: one subscription for breadth, one API account for what gets built into their own software.

How much does the Claude API cost?

It's billed per token, split into input and output, and the price depends on the model you choose. The bill isn't driven by the number of users but by text length, model choice and number of steps. Two complete software jobs in my company came to 52 and 114 US dollars in model costs. Prompt caching is the biggest single saving.

Does Claude work in German?

Yes, fully. The quality of German copy depends less on the model than on the brief: give it example sentences, sentence lengths and a list of banned words, otherwise you get ad-speak.

Can I put customer data into Claude?

Only with a data processing agreement, an entry in your records of processing and as little personal data as possible. In practice that means replacing names and numbers beforehand and processing call content locally where you can. A binding assessment comes from a data protection officer, not from me.

What is Claude Code?

Claude in the terminal, with access to files, Git and commands. Instead of handing you code snippets, it works on a real project: multiple files, tests, an explanation of the change. That's what I used to build my CRM, my websites and my video pipeline.

Do I need to know how to code to use Claude Code?

To build something: no. To understand what got built: yes, at least enough to read code. If you can't, don't let it near real customer data – not because of the model, but because nobody will notice when it quietly does the wrong thing.
